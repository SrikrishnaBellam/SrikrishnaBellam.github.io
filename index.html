Modeling and Classifying Parkinsonian Tremors With Nonlinear Dynamics and Kalman Optimization

V. Vanitha, V. Dhilip Kumar, O. Geman, O. Postolache, S. K. Bellam, R. Toderean

Estimates Parkinsonian rest-tremor frequency from noisy clinical video. An Extended Lagrangian model captures the tremor's irregular oscillation (Rayleigh dissipation, nonlinear stiffness, external forcing), and Kalman filtering suppresses recording noise. Adaptive Nyquist-compliant sparse sampling keeps it light enough for resource-limited settings. The extracted features then feed a 1D CNN-LSTM that grades disease severity, which points toward remote assessment of patients.

Advanced Kalman Filtering for Video-Based Tremor Assessment in Parkinson's Disease: Comparative Analysis with Deep Learning Classification

V. Vanitha, M. Srivani, S. K. Bellam, S. K. Mohanavel

Compares three state-space filters (KF, EKF and UKF) for denoising fingertip trajectories tracked with MediaPipe across 60 tremor videos. The UKF suppresses noise best, and its filtered signals feed a CNN-LSTM that classifies UPDRS tremor severity (0–3) at 96.7% precision and 96.9% recall, pointing toward contactless remote assessment.
